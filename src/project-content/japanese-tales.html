Where Stories Take Root - the Landscapes Behind Japanese Tales

The main idea of this map is to show Japanese traditional stories on a 3D map. Many traditional stories have a close connection to their place of origin, as they are often shaped by the local geography, culture, and history. For example, the background of the story of Urashima Taro is set by the sea, which aligns with its place of origin, the Seto Inland Sea.

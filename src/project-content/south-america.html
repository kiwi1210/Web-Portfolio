Military Dictatorship in South America

After watching the Oscar-winning Brazilian film 'Ainda Estou Aqui (I Am Still Here),' we realized how little is widely known about the military dictatorships that shaped much of South America. Between the 1960s and 1980s, almost every country in the region experienced an authoritarian regime marked by censorship, torture, and the forced disappearance of thousands of people.

As part of a web mapping and multimedia cartography project in our International Erasmus Mundus M.Sc. Cartography program, we developed a website that explores these dark chapters of South American history. We collected books, songs, and films that give voice to victims and resistance movements, and designed an interactive resource to help more people connect with this shared past.

Our goal is not only to remember, but to reflect on how these histories still resonate in today’s struggles for human rights, truth, and democracy across the region.

Highlights of our project:

  • Thorough content — we put a lot of care into researching and organizing information to make it clear and accessible.
  • Unique design — we went for a black-and-white newspaper look, with moving elements like a slideshow and an animated SVG map, think Harry Potter newspaper vibes!
  • Interactive features — the map and text panels respond to each other, making it easy to explore both history and geography together.
